BIOGRAPHY

Jiarui is now a visiting research scholar at University College London, hosted by Prof. Jun Wang, Prof. Yali Du (King's College London) and Prof. Yuanchang Liu. He is also a Zhiyuan Honor Ph.D. Student in Wu Honor Class, Computer Science Department, Shanghai Jiao Tong University starting from Autumn 2019. His graduate research advisors are Prof. Yong Yu, Prof. Weinan Zhang and Prof. Jun Wang (University College London).

Previously, he was a research intern or an applied scientist intern at Amazon Web Services Shanghai AI-Labs, Taobao Live, Alimama, DiDi AI-Labs, and closely collaborated with China Merchants Bank. He earned his B.Eng. from Yingcai Honor Class of University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in 2019.

Jiarui's research interests include machine learning and data mining, especially, deep learning algorithms and applications in real-world data mining scenarios with structured data such as sequences and graphs.
