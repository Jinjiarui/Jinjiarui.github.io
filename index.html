BIOGRAPHY

Jiarui is a Zhiyuan Honor Ph.D. Student at Apex Data and Knowledge Management Lab and Wu Honor Class, Computer Science Department, Shanghai Jiao Tong University starting from September 2019. His graduate research advisors are Prof. Yong Yu, Prof. Weinan Zhang and Prof. Jun Wang (University College London).

Previously, Jiarui was a visiting student researcher at Artificial Intelligence Center, University College London. He was a research intern or an applied scientist intern at Amazon Web Services Shanghai AI-Labs, Taobao Live, Alimama, DiDi AI-Labs, and closely collaborated with China Merchants Bank. He earned his B.Eng. from Yingcai Honor Class of University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in 2019.

Jiarui's academic background and industrial internships mainly focus on how to leverage (past) temporal behaviors (a.k.a., sequence data) from a user to predict her (current) needs. Welcome to check Research Summary. Jiarui is on Job Market for full-time industrial position.
